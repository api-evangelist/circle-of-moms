# Circle of Moms

Circle of Moms was an online community for mothers, organized around topical mom groups, discussion
threads and mom blogs, covering pregnancy, babies and infants, parenting and family health.

**Status: defunct — no API surface.** The circleofmoms.com domain is still registered but redirects to
the POPSUGAR Family page on Facebook, and its DNS is administered under Vox Media (POPSUGAR's parent).
Circle of Moms never published a public API, developer portal, SDK, or partner platform: web-archive
crawls of the domain contain no developer, API-documentation, or OAuth paths, and no `developer.` or
`api.` subdomain has ever been recorded.

This profile is retained as a historical company record.
