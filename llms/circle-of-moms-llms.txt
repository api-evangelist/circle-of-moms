# Circle of Moms

> Circle of Moms was an online community for mothers built around topical mom groups, discussion
> threads and mom blogs. It is no longer operating. The company is a portfolio company of Uncork
> Capital and its site was later operated under the POPSUGAR brand. Circle of Moms never published a
> public API, developer portal, SDK, or partner platform.

## Status

- Operating status: defunct — no live product or developer surface as of 2026-07-20
- circleofmoms.com: still registered (created 2006-05-01, Amazon Registrar), redirects to the POPSUGAR
  Family page on Facebook (https://www.facebook.com/PopSugarFamily/)
- DNS/email administered under Vox Media (DMARC `rua` delegates to g9.dmarc.voxmedia.com); MX on
  Google Workspace
- No `developer.circleofmoms.com` or `api.circleofmoms.com` host resolves
- No developer, API-documentation, OAuth, or partner-platform path appears in web-archive crawls of
  the domain
